how it works

  1. 1

    add the bot

    invite meow bot to your server. it needs permission to read messages and send messages.

  2. 2

    say meow

    type meow, mreow, mew, mrow, mrrp, nya, or purr. the bot will notice.

  3. 3

    receive meow

    it replies with a meow. it also counts the meow, because someone has to keep score.

in the wild

teach it new sounds

server admins can add custom regex triggers with /config. give it a name and a pattern, and the bot will meow when the pattern matches. patterns are tested before they are saved.

good to know

  • meow and woof are built in. woof is off by default.
  • replies are limited to 5 per 5 seconds per channel.
  • the bot does not store message content.

how the meowing works

  1. 1

    bring the cat home

    invite meow bot to your server. it needs permission to read messages and send messages.

  2. 2

    say a cat noise

    type meow, mreow, mew, mrow, mrrp, nya, or purr. the cat will notice.

  3. 3

    get a meow back

    it replies with a meow. it also counts the meow, because someone has to keep score.

field notes

caught in the wild

a rare sighting of the meow bot in its natural habitat. the cat is very fast.

one meow goes in. one meow comes out. the tally moves by one.

teach an old cat new sounds

server admins can add custom triggers with /config. pick a word or phrase in simple mode, or a regex in advanced mode, and give each trigger its own reply. patterns are tested before they are saved.

the cat is a fast learner. the cat is also picky about regex.

good to know

  • meow and woof are built in. woof is off by default.
  • replies are limited to 5 per 5 seconds per channel.
  • the bot does not store message content.
  • admins can allow or deny channels with /config.
